Entre Deux Mondes

Written and directed by Ahmed Mokrane

An immigrant student in Montréal, building a new life while still tied to the one he left.

The film treats immigration as an emotional journey as much as a geographic one: the feeling of living between two realities at once. Identity, belonging and the quiet weight of distance.

How a project moves

  1. Understand

    The story, the message, the audience and the identity behind the project.

  2. Imagine

    A visual direction built from references, light, composition, movement and atmosphere.

  3. Create

    Preparation on set, with room left for the moments nobody planned.

  4. Shape

    Edit and grade, every frame kept faithful to the original intention.

  5. Make it feel

    Technical quality is never the goal. The audience should feel something.

About

A life behind the lens. It started when he was three.

  1. 01 · Origins · Algeria

    Photography first

    Growing up in Algeria, photography was his first way of seeing: people, emotion, and the chance to keep a moment alive beyond its time. Photography taught him to observe. Filmmaking taught him to feel, move and tell stories.

  2. 02 · Turning point · Canada

    Starting over

    Moving to Canada meant risk, sacrifice and building from the ground up. Through the doubt, the camera stayed constant. One project led to the next, and a passion became a profession.

  3. 03 · Today · Montréal

    Director & Cinematographer

    Visual stories across film, live events, music and brands, with one rule: every frame needs intention, emotion and a reason to exist.
