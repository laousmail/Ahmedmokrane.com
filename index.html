    Sc. 08Philosophy

    The camera is a language.

    No two projects should look the same. Every story, artist and brief gets its own visual grammar.

    Lightcreatesemotion
    Compositioncreatesintention
    Movementcreatesenergy
    Editingcreatesrhythm

    Every project starts with one question

    What should the audience feel when they see this?

    Before cameras, lenses or equipment, the work starts with the story and the intention behind it. On a short film, a music video or a festival stage, he looks past the image for five things:

    an emotionan energya momenta connectiona memory

    How a project moves

    1. Understand

      The story, the message, the audience and the identity behind the project.

    2. Imagine

      A visual direction built from references, light, composition, movement and atmosphere.

    3. Create

      Preparation on set, with room left for the moments nobody planned.

    4. Shape

      Edit and grade, every frame kept faithful to the original intention.

    5. Make it feel

      Technical quality is never the goal. The audience should feel something.

    About

    A life behind the lens. It started when he was three years old.

    1. Origins · Algeria

      Photography first

      Growing up in Algeria, photography was his first way of seeing: people, emotion, and the chance to keep a moment alive beyond its time.

      Photography taught him to observe. Filmmaking taught him to feel, move and tell stories.

    2. The turning point · Canada

      Starting over

      Moving to Canada meant risk, sacrifice and building from the ground up. Through the doubt, the camera stayed constant. One project led to the next, and a passion became a profession.

    3. Today · Montréal

      Director & Cinematographer

      Visual stories across film, live events, music and brands, with one rule: every frame needs intention, emotion and a reason to exist.
